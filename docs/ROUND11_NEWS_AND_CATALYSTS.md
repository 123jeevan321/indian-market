Summary: of 12 checked moves, **8 followed quarterly results**, 1 a large company investment announcement, 2 a sector-wide or commodity
move, and 1 has no confirmed cause. The window (28 Sep - 11 Nov) is Q2 results season, which explains the concentration.

What this means for prediction:
- A result is a scheduled event (the board-meeting date is filed in advance), but its CONTENT is not. Knowing the date predicts a
  big move in either direction (PPLPHARMA +17 %, PIIND -19 %), not which way. This matches Phase 2: pre-move data predicts size, not sign.
- When results come after hours, the move is mostly in the opening auction price (Phase 3, type A); nobody can buy before it.
- When results come during the session, the opening looks ordinary and the move starts after the announcement (type B). Reacting to
  the announcement within minutes is a news-trading problem, not a pattern-in-the-chart problem, and needs a live filings feed that
  this project does not have.
- No news published after a move was used to make any prediction in this round.
